# Research note style

- English only. Plain words, short sentences, no filler.
- Every figure carries its source and a grade: R reported (search summary of a primary), S secondary, I inference (state inputs), H hypothesis. Mark anything not opened at the source UNVERIFIED.
- Separate what is sourced from what is our judgement. List gaps and conflicts openly.
- Put requirements in tables with IDs, priority (P0/P1/P2) and built / partly / not built, checked against the repository.
- End with open decisions for the founder and suggested defaults.
- Save in `research_notes/<topic>/` (notes) or `reports/` (final write-ups); log it in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
